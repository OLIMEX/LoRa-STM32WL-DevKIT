Notice that the hardware design of BB-STM32WL is not open source hardware (unline the design of LoRa-STM32WL-DevKit).

We provide only the schematic for BB-STM32WL, not the design files.

- Hardware revision D
===

1. Some components moved to the bottom;
2. Major improvements of the design;
3. Added TXCO option.

- Hardware revision D1 - major improvements to the design and high band and high power
===

1. Fixed the antenna part, now works properly at both 868 and 915 highband and highpower modes;
2. Changed the 32mhz crystal to a better one;
3. C6 and C7 of 32khz crystal now 10pf for more reliable start up.
